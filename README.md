# E-Commerce Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis & K-Means Clustering)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 4,870 historical transactions from a UK-based e-commerce store. By applying **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** metrics and the **K-Means Clustering** machine learning algorithm, I segmented 1,932 unique customers into actionable business profiles to drive targeted marketing strategies.

## Objective
Transition from traditional mass marketing to a personalized, data-driven approach to maximize ROI and customer retention.

## Tools & Tech Stack
* **Language:** Python (Pandas, NumPy)
* **Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Machine Learning:** Scikit-Learn (K-Means, StandardScaler)
* **Presentation:** Business Intelligence Deck (PDF)

## Key Insights & Recommendations
1. **Market Dominance:** The UK market generates >80% of total revenue. **Action:** Optimize UK supply chain and fulfillment logistics.
2. **VIP Retention:** Identified 9 'Champion' customers driving the highest average monetary value ($2,446). **Action:** Implement a tiered loyalty program.
3. **Reactivation Strategy:** Found 660 'At-Risk' customers with a high lapse in recency (avg. 252 days). **Action:** Launch aggressive, time-sensitive email marketing campaigns.
